
## General Questions

### **1. What is the ambiance of the restaurant?**
Bistro offers a warm, modern, and inviting atmosphere. We aim for a "smart casual" vibe – perfect for a romantic dinner, a celebratory meal with friends, or a sophisticated business lunch. The lighting is intimate in the evenings, and the decor features contemporary art and comfortable seating.

### **2. Do I need a reservation?**
Reservations are **highly recommended**, especially for dinner service from Thursday to Saturday and for our weekend brunch. However, we always keep a few tables and seats at the bar available for walk-in guests. These are offered on a first-come, first-served basis. You can book a table through **[our website](link-to-website)** or by calling us directly at **(555) 123-4567**.

### **3. What are your opening hours?**
Our hours of operation are:
* **Lunch:** Monday - Friday, 12:00 PM - 2:30 PM
* **Dinner:** Monday - Saturday, 5:30 PM - 10:00 PM (Last seating at 9:45 PM)
* **Weekend Brunch:** Saturday & Sunday, 11:00 AM - 3:00 PM
* **Closed:** We are closed on Sundays for dinner service.

_Please note: Holiday hours may vary. Check our website or social media channels for special announcements._

### **4. Where are you located and is there parking?**
📍 We are located at **123 Main Street, Downtown**.
🚗 There is paid metered parking available on Main Street and adjacent side streets. Additionally, a large public parking garage is located one block away on Market Avenue, which is often the most convenient option.

### **5. Is your restaurant accessible?** ♿
Yes, our restaurant is fully accessible. We have a ramp at the main entrance for wheelchair access, and our main dining room and restrooms are located on the ground floor and are ADA compliant. Please let us know when booking if you have any specific accessibility needs so we can ensure your comfort.

## Reservations: Changes & Cancellations

### **1. How can I make or change my reservation?**
The easiest way to make or modify a reservation is through the link provided in your confirmation email or by visiting the "Reservations" tab on our website. For same-day changes or if you need immediate assistance, please call the restaurant directly at **(555) 123-4567**. All changes are subject to availability.

### **2. What is your cancellation policy?**
We kindly ask for at least **24 hours' notice** for any cancellations.
* For reservations of **6 or more people**, a credit card is required to hold the table.
* A fee of **$25 per person** will be charged to the card on file for no-shows or cancellations made within **12 hours** of the reservation time.

### **3. What happens if I'm running late for my reservation?**
We understand that delays can happen! We will hold your table for a grace period of **15 minutes**. If you anticipate being later than 15 minutes, please call us to let us know. While we will do our best to accommodate you upon your arrival, we may need to release the table or offer you the next available spot.

### **4. Can I request a specific table or seating area?**
You may add a note with your seating preference (e.g., "window table," "quiet corner," "booth") when making a reservation online or mention it when calling. While we **cannot guarantee** specific tables due to the flow of service, our host team will always do their very best to accommodate your request.

### **5. Do you confirm reservations?**
Yes. You will receive an email or SMS confirmation shortly after booking. We also send a reminder message 24-48 hours before your reservation, which we ask you to confirm.

## Menu & Dietary Needs

### **1. Where can I find your current menu?**
Our current menus are always available on our website under the "Menu" tab. We have separate menus for Lunch, Dinner, Brunch, and Desserts, as well as our full Wine & Cocktail list.
*Please note that dishes may change based on the availability of fresh, seasonal ingredients.*

### **2. Do you accommodate dietary restrictions and allergies?**
Absolutely. We take dietary needs very seriously.
* We have dedicated vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free options clearly marked on our menu.
* Please inform your server of **any allergies or dietary restrictions** when you order.
* For severe allergies (e.g., celiac disease, severe nut allergies), we recommend contacting us at least 48 hours in advance so our culinary team can take all necessary precautions to prevent cross-contamination.

### **3. Do you have a children's menu?**
Yes, we offer a dedicated menu for our younger guests (ages 12 and under). We also have high chairs and booster seats available upon request. We aim to be a family-friendly establishment.

### **4. Are your ingredients locally sourced?**
We are proud to partner with local farms and purveyors to source the freshest seasonal ingredients possible. Our menu reflects our commitment to quality and supporting our local community. Ask your server about the daily specials, which often highlight what's best in the market right now!

## Payments & Billing

### **1. What payment methods do you accept?**
We accept cash and all major credit cards, including Visa, MasterCard, and American Express. We also accept modern contactless payments like Apple Pay and Google Pay. We do not accept personal checks.

### **2. Can we split the bill?**
Yes, we are happy to split the bill for your table. For ease and accuracy of service, we can split a bill up to **four** equal ways. If you require more complicated splits or separate checks, please inform your server at the beginning of your meal.

### **3. Is a service charge or gratuity included in the bill?**
* For parties of **eight or more**, an automatic gratuity of **20%** will be added to the final bill to ensure our staff is fairly compensated for their hard work.
* For all other parties, gratuity is left to your discretion.

### **4. Do you sell or accept gift cards?**
Yes! Gift cards for Bistro are the perfect gift. They can be purchased in person at the restaurant or as e-gift cards through our website. They can be used for any food and beverage purchases and they never expire.

## Groups & Special Occasions

### **1. How can I book for a large group?**
For parties of **nine or more** people, please use the "Group Booking" form on our website or call us directly. To ensure a smooth and timely dining experience for your group, we offer special set menus and family-style dining options.

### **2. Can I bring my own cake for a celebration? 🎂**
Yes, you are welcome to bring your own cake for special occasions. We charge a "cakeage" fee of **$5 per person**, which covers storage, cutting, and plating the cake with a scoop of vanilla ice cream for your guests.

### **3. Can I bring my own wine? What is the corkage fee? 🍷**
Yes, you may bring your own wine. Our corkage fee is **$30 per 750ml bottle**, with a maximum of two bottles per table. We kindly request that the wine you bring is not a vintage that we currently offer on our own wine list.

### **4. Do you host private events?**
Yes, Bistro is available for private events, semi-private functions, and full restaurant buyouts. Our space is perfect for corporate dinners, wedding rehearsals, holiday parties, and more. Please visit the "Private Events" section of our website to see our packages and contact our dedicated events coordinator.

### **5. Can I bring decorations for my table?**
You are welcome to bring small, tasteful decorations like balloons (weighted) or small floral arrangements for your celebration. To ensure the comfort of all our guests, we do not permit confetti, glitter, or open flames (other than birthday candles).

## Amenities & Policies

### **1. What is the dress code?**
Our dress code is **smart casual**. We want you to be comfortable, but we do ask that guests refrain from wearing athletic wear, shorts, or flip-flops in the main dining room during dinner service.

### **2. Do you offer Wi-Fi?**
Yes, we offer complimentary Wi-Fi for our guests. Please ask your server for the network name and password.

### **3. Are pets allowed?**
We love animals, but in accordance with health code regulations, only certified service animals are permitted inside the restaurant. Our outdoor patio seating (when available seasonally) is pet-friendly.

### **4. Lost & Found**
If you believe you have left an item behind, please call us at **(555) 123-4567**. We hold found items for 30 days. Bistro is not responsible for any lost or stolen items.
